# One-Year New Testament Chronological Bible Study

Welcome to a year-long journey through the entire New Testament in chronological order. Over 52 weeks, you will read all 260 chapters of the New Testament — not in the order they appear in your Bible, but in the order the events actually happened and the letters were actually written.

> **"All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness."** — 2 Timothy 3:16
